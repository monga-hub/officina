# Approved Officina 1907 artwork

Web derivatives of the approved print compositions and Card-a-mon revision 21 (75 cards), exported through PDF composition and rendering. Source names, SHA-256 hashes, crop coordinates and card IDs are in `manifest.json`.

- Hexagonal symbols identify resources; circular symbols identify departments.
- Portrait crops reproduce Card-a-mon field transforms, including the corrected Spanish chemistry portrait.
- `main-board.jpg` contains the complete physical board on a 6253 × 4321 coordinate canvas. The source export omitted the right and bottom card spaces; a registered industrial city extension supplies their background. The original star body, chains, sector colors and labels come from the user’s original high-resolution PNG, preserved through PDF clipping. Six node medallions and five coins reuse the exact approved icon masters. Approved calendar, Welfare and seven policy symbols retain their existing PDF image objects. The original round dial and title plate are retained with natural PDF clipping paths.
- The five printed price numerals are omitted only in the web derivative; legal entry costs remain live HTML overlays. Five live market cards occupy the physical card bays.
- The three `*-texture.jpg` files come from the original player board. They form stacked Tessile, Chimica and Metallurgia strips with live serpentine and tension tracks, including custom presets. The rejected player-board redesign is not used.
- `amber.jpg` is blank amber glass from the approved calendar, used when a custom game needs different turn numbers.
- Coin values, card effects, fund balances and player markers remain live React elements.

The local source/proof packages are the `officina-rondella-icons` and `officina-original-star` task archives. `officina-original-star/composition.json` records the restored star, placement coordinates and source hashes. The previous complete-board registration and exterior imagery are preserved; an ImageGen clean plate is clipped to the obsolete machine footprint underneath the restored original star. Source masters and the external Card-a-mon document remain unchanged.

Purchase access matches the printed card bays: A Tessile–Metallurgia, B Metallurgia–Chimica, C Chimica–Repressione, D Repressione–Welfare and E Welfare–Tessile. The shared market mapping drives legal purchases, card details and highlights. Saved games with the former lab mapping adopt these C/E links, including Undo history, without changing cards or prices; custom and legacy mappings remain intact.
